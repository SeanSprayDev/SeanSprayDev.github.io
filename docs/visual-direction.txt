Portfolio Visual Motif Sheet

Theme:
Dark celestial / moonlit developer archive.

Core feeling:
Curious, structured, quiet, mysterious, technical.

Main symbol:
Orbiting moon glyph.
Used in hero, favicon/logo idea, footer, and maybe project card backs.

Supporting shapes:
Circle — systems, cycles, worlds.
Crescent — moonlight, curiosity, mystery.
Triangle — direction, decisions, progress.
Square — structure, tools, architecture.
Diamond — projects, artifacts, case files.
Nodes/orbit lines — connections, systems thinking.

Texture:
Subtle grain/noise.
Faint celestial chart marks.
No parchment, no brown fantasy scroll look.

Motion:
Slow orbit on moon glyph.
Maybe one or two slow rotating shapes.
Hover effects should be subtle.
No fast spinning, bouncing, flashing, or distracting animation.

Accent behavior:
Teal is the main accent.
Use teal for buttons, title lines, hover states, card edges, and glyph highlights.
Other colors only if project-specific.
